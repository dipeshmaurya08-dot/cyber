Curriculum & Guidelines

Student Cyber Safety Knowledge Hub

Practical, actionable cybersecurity habits curated specifically for students living in hostels, using campus Wi-Fi, and navigating online academic life.

Module 1

College Phishing & Scholarship Frauds

Fraudsters impersonate college exam authorities, university registrars, and fake CSR scholarship foundations to harvest login credentials and advance fees.

Always check for legitimate domain endings (.ac.in or .edu.in).
Never transfer "processing fees" for internships or government scholarships.
Module 2

The Fortress Strategy: 2FA & Passwords

Password reuse is the single biggest cause of student account takeovers. A leak on an online gaming forum shouldn't compromise your college portal.

Adopt free open-source password managers like Bitwarden or Google Keychain.
Switch from SMS OTP to app-based authenticators (immune to SIM swapping).
Module 3

Campus Wi-Fi & Public Network Hygiene

Open Wi-Fi at college cafeterias, libraries, and hostels can be exploited via "Evil Twin" hotspots that mimic legitimate network SSIDs.

Use a trusted VPN or Cloudflare 1.1.1.1 WARP when browsing on public routers.
Never perform UPI transfers or bank logins on open coffee shop networks.
Module 4

Piracy, Modded APKs & Infostealers

Cracked CAD software, modded Spotify/Insta APKs, and torrented textbooks frequently bundle RedLine or Lumma infostealers that harvest browser session cookies.

Claim free student developer packages (GitHub Student Pack, JetBrains, Autodesk).
Never disable Windows Defender or antivirus to run cracked .exe files.
Module 5

Social Media Privacy & Oversharing

Posting exam admit cards, student IDs, flight boarding passes, or real-time hostel locations allows stalkers and fraudsters to forge identity credentials.

Practice delayed posting for vacations and college events.
Never post images revealing barcode/QR codes from admit cards or ID badges.
Module 6

3-2-1 Backup Rule for Academic Work

Protect your thesis, assignments, and capstone project code against hardware disk crashes, laptop theft, and ransomware deadlines.

3 copies of your work across 2 different media, with 1 offsite in cloud storage.
Push code frequently to private GitHub/GitLab repositories.
Emergency Incident Response

Hacked or Scammed? Act Within The Golden Hour

If money has been debited via unauthorized UPI or your student email / social accounts are compromised, taking these immediate steps can stop financial loss and preserve evidence.

1
Call 1930 Helpline Immediately: Dial the National Cyber Crime Helpline (India) to initiate financial freeze on scammer mule accounts.
2
Freeze UPI & Debit Cards: Open your banking mobile app and toggle "Disable Online Transactions / Temporary Block".
3
Preserve Digital Evidence: Take full screenshots showing timestamps, transaction IDs, phone numbers, and full email headers before blocking the scammer.
4
File Official Police Complaint: Register formal cyber crime complaint on cybercrime.gov.in and notify your college IT cell.
